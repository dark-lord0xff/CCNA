Lab: Ethernet Cabling and Fiber

What I learned:
- Without Auto MDI-X, same device types require crossover cables.
- Different device types generally use straight-through cables.
- Copper Ethernet has a typical maximum segment length of 100 m.
- Multimode fiber supports longer distances than copper.
- Single-mode fiber supports longer distances than multimode.

Ethernet pinout:
- MDI (PC/Router): TX = 1,2 | RX = 3,6
- MDI-X (Switch): TX = 3,6 | RX = 1,2
